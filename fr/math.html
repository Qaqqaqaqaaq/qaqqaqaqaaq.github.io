Two short proofs of incompatibility for correlation matrices

arXiv:2609.14610 math.ST, math.PR September 2026

Fix a standardised distribution F. Which correlation matrices can be realised by a random vector all of whose margins are F? The achievable set sits inside the elliptope of all correlation matrices, and this note shows the containment can be strict. Two short arguments do the work: an eleven-term representation of |x|4 as a sum of fourth powers of linear forms settles the uniform case in dimension 11, and the six diagonals of a regular icosahedron settle the arcsine case in dimension 6. Combined with an earlier result of Devroye and Letac, this pins equality to exactly the dimensions d ≤ 5, confirming their conjecture.
