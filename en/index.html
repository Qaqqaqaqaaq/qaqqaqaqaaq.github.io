Applied probability & statistics

I graduated in Applied Mathematics from McGill University. My academic focus is in mathematical statistics, probability theory, and complex analysis.

I'm currently a student at the Politecnico di Milano (PoliMi), where I'm doing an MSc in Mathematical Engineering and studying statistical learning. For my undergraduate Honours thesis, I worked under Prof. Nešlehová and Prof. Valiquette on modelling multivariate Bernoulli variables using tree structures.

Beyond my studies, I enjoy the intersections of mathematics and other fields, including spatial statistics in GIS, and probabilistic methods in historical linguistics. In my free time, I'm learning Italian, study the Hindustani and Salishan languages, construct my own languages, play Blood on the Clocktower with friends, drink lots of tea, and design ski resorts.
